folder6. Documents, Files, and Local Storage

Our application implements a decentralized, client-first document storage architecture:

  • Browser IndexedDB (studygen_pdf_db): Your scanned camera photos, page crops, and generated PDF binary files are saved directly on your local device in browser IndexedDB under the documents store.
  • No Cloud PDF Storage: EasyScan does NOT upload, host, or store your PDF files or raw document images in our MongoDB cloud database.
  • Local Document Deletion: You have full control over your local documents. You can delete any document at any time using the in-app three-dot menu (⋮) -> Delete action, which immediately erases the document and its binary pages from your device.

smart_toy7. AI Features and AI Output Disclaimer

EasyScan includes artificial intelligence features to assist with document revision and study:

  • AI Provider & Model: AI functionality is powered exclusively by the Groq AI API using the openai/gpt-oss-120b language model. Gemini is NOT the active AI provider in EasyScan.
  • Text-Only Processing: When AI features are invoked, only extracted document text and user prompts are transmitted to Groq over HTTPS. Binary PDF files are not uploaded to the AI provider.
  • Temporary Processing: Files uploaded for server-side text extraction are temporarily held in backend/temp_uploads/ (max 25 MB) and deleted immediately upon extraction in a finally block, supported by a 15-minute cron with 10-minute TTL cleanup.
  • AI Output Disclaimer: AI-generated summaries, study notes, quiz questions, flashcards, and chat responses are generated algorithmically. They are provided solely on an "AS IS" basis for study assistance. AI outputs may occasionally contain inaccuracies, hallucinations, or incomplete explanations. You are solely responsible for independently verifying all AI output before relying upon it. EasyScan does not provide certified legal, medical, financial, or professional advice.
